# Project Overview

## Scientific Question

How do vegetation, velocity structure, and particle properties influence microplastic transport, retention, and breakthrough behavior in channel flow?

## Workflow

1. Experimental measurements provide velocity profiles and tracer or particle observations.
2. Hydrodynamic simulations are verified against measured velocity profiles.
3. Particle tracking models use the verified flow fields to simulate microplastic motion.
4. Static figures summarize velocity, concentration, and breakthrough behavior.
5. Animations communicate particle motion and evolving distributions over time.

## Suggested GitHub Narrative

- The research motivation and physical setting.

## Figure Ideas

- Channel geometry schematic.
- Experimental versus simulated velocity profiles.
- Particle trajectory maps.
- Breakthrough curves at downstream stations.
- Sensitivity comparison across velocity, vegetation, or particle cases.

## Animation Ideas

- Time-evolving particle positions in the channel.
- Concentration plume movement along the channel.
- Side-by-side animation comparing dye and microplastic transport.
- Velocity profile build-up or model verification sequence.
