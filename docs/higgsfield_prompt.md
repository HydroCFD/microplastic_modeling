# Higgsfield Creative Prompt

Use this prompt in Higgsfield Image, Video, Cinema Studio, or Shorts Studio to create a high-end visual asset for the repository page.

## Concept

Create a cinematic scientific visualization of microplastic transport in a turbulent water channel.

## Prompt

Wide cinematic underwater cutaway of an open-channel flow carrying microplastic particles through clear blue-green water. The water moves left to right with elegant glowing streamlines, vortices, wake structures, and turbulent eddies. Small bright microplastic particles of different sizes and colors are suspended through the water column, some near the free surface and some mixing through the depth. Add subtle rigid vegetation stems emerging from the bed, showing how vegetation modifies turbulence and particle paths. The bed has realistic sand and small gravel. The image should feel like a high-end environmental hydraulics research visualization, polished, modern, scientific, and visually striking.

No text, no labels, no watermark, no people, no animals, no plastic bottles, no trash piles. Keep it credible and clean, not cartoonish. Use luminous aquatic blues, teal, cyan, white highlights, and small amber/magenta particle accents.

## Video Motion Direction

Camera slowly pushes forward through the transparent water volume while microplastic particles drift downstream. Streamlines and vortices gently pulse. Particles should follow curved trajectories around wake regions and vegetation stems. Keep motion smooth, elegant, and suitable for a GitHub project banner or short research teaser.

## Suggested Output

- Landscape banner: `16:9`
- Duration: `5-8 s`
- Style: cinematic scientific 3D visualization
- Use on GitHub as a GIF or short MP4 preview
